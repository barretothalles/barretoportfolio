CASE 01

Pricing & packaging for an AI SaaS

UX PILOT · 2026PRICINGPACKAGINGVALUE COMMUNICATION

Free signups were growing while paid conversion stayed flat, and the gap was widest outside the product's core markets.

CONTEXT

UX Pilot serves a global base of designers on a freemium model. Cohort reports showed healthy activation with weak upgrade rates in several regions, and session recordings pointed to hesitation on the pricing page while the product itself stayed well rated.

HYPOTHESIS & EXPERIMENT

A single global price ignored purchasing power and how each segment described value. I ran a price elasticity study, mapped willingness to pay by region, then shipped geo-based pricing with repackaged plans and rewrote the value communication on pricing surfaces for each segment.

RESULT

Free-to-paid conversion rose 34.8% against the previous cycle, with churn and LTV guardrails tracked in dedicated cohort reports to confirm the gain held up as healthy revenue.

WHAT I LEARNEDPrice is a message. The framing of value moved behavior as much as the number itself.

CASE 02

Go-to-market for Nodey, an AI agent

UX PILOT · 2026POSITIONINGCOMPETITIVE INTELLAUNCH

An AI agent for Figma entering a market that Google Stitch and fast-moving design tools were reshaping every month.

MARKET & ICP

I mapped the landscape into a competitive matrix, ran a full teardown of Google Stitch, and tested the product hands-on for weeks. From there I defined the ICP and the jobs to be done that Nodey wins on, where deep workflow automation matters more than raw generation.

POSITIONING & MESSAGING

The positioning statement anchored Nodey as the agent that works inside real design files. I built the messaging hierarchy from hook to proof, then turned it into launch assets, landing narrative, demo scripts and creator briefs.

DISTRIBUTION & SIGNALS

Launch distribution ran through the creator engine described in case 03, giving the narrative reach from day one. Early signals include consistent message adoption across channels and demo-driven signups tracked in PostHog.

CASE 03

Product-led creator distribution

UX PILOT · 2025 TO 2026PROGRAM DESIGN0→1$300K+ MANAGED

Paid acquisition was expensive and the product demos beautifully in expert hands, so I designed distribution as a permanent program.

ARCHITECTURE

I designed a three-tier program with clear entry criteria, from community ambassadors to flagship experts. Each tier has its own briefing template that translates product use cases into hooks, proof points and publishing formats.

OPERATING SYSTEM

Outreach playbooks, a proprietary KPI framework and a tracking system keep seven community channels running at once, with performance reporting that feeds back into briefs every cycle.

RESULT

The program reached 8M+ organic impressions on X and Instagram, scaled to $300K+ in managed campaigns, and now runs as a repeatable system that carried the Nodey launch in case 02.
